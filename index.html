    How to play
    How scoring works

    The basics

    • Country: 50 points for naming it. Pin: up to 50, more the closer it lands.
    • Perfect: the right country with your pin inside it earns a +10 bonus, multiplied like the rest. Perfects raise your rank, from Tourist to Atlas.
    • Streak: each right answer in a row multiplies the next one by 1.5, up to ×4. Two-choice rounds (higher or lower, north or south) pay half for the name and count half a step.
    • The final question counts double. Medium pays ×1.25, Hard ×1.5 and Expert ×2.
    • Clues are optional. Each takes a percentage off that question's points. After Eliminate a wrong answer, Eliminate another clears a second one for a bigger cut.
    • Find it: just the pin. Small countries count from 500 km away; big ones need a closer pin and pay a bit less.
    • Tricky facts that most players miss pay up to ×1.25.
    • Countries you miss come back in later rounds until you get them right.

    Hot streaks

    • Fever: five perfects in a row and your next three questions pay × 2.
    • Bonus round: 10 right in a row on Easy (9 on Hard, 8 on Expert) unlocks three timed bonus questions, after a 3-second countdown. Run out of time and you can continue once, for half your bonus points.
    • Free spin: a perfect round, every pin inside the right country, earns one extra question with a mystery reward.
    • Prize chest: play past the end of a round (a bonus round or a free spin) and you pick one of three chests.

    Luck

    • Mystery multiplier: before the final question a slot spins: × 1, × 2, × 3 or, rarely, × 5.
    • Jackpot: one secret country a day pays × 5 if you name it. Lucky multipliers together top out at × 5.
    • Shared jackpot: a pool for everyone playing today. It grows with every game, and the first perfect pin on the secret jackpot country wins it all.
    • Golden landmarks: sometimes a gold coin hides on the globe. Tap it before you lock in. Three in a round unlock the bonus round; in Survival, three earn an extra life.
    • Bets: after each answer you can stake up to half your points on the next one.

    Power-ups and rewards

    • Power-ups: everyone gets a free ↻ Second chance each day. Beyond that, a perfect answer drops one half the time, and so does every 1,000 points in a game, up to 3 a game. Hold up to three; unused ones carry over. Hover over a symbol to see what it does: ❄ Freeze (stops the clock for 10 seconds, timed games only), 🔍 Lens, ½ 50/50, 🛡 Shield (a wrong answer keeps your streak), 🧭 Compass (the continent, free), ×2 Double up, ↻ Second chance (right on the retry, the country pays half) and ♥ Extra life (Survival). Luck, Fever and Double up together top out at × 8.
    • Daily login: claim power-ups once a day along a 7-day row that builds to three on day 7. Miss one day and it waits for you.
    • Passport sets: stamp every place on a continent to complete its set, for a badge and two power-ups.

    Levels and Survival

    • Levels: Easy asks about well-known countries, takes its wrong choices from other continents, counts a pin within 300 km as perfect and has no clock. Medium takes its choices from the same continent. Hard and Expert use the nearest countries and score pins more strictly. Expert leans toward small places, allows one clue per question and always has the clock.
    • Survival: endless questions and three lives. The clock gets shorter every three questions.
